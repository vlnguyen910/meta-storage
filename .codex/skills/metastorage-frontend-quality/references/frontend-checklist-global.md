# Frontend checklist focus

Prioritize high-signal findings from the local Front-End Checklist corpus:

- every form control has a visible label and useful autocomplete semantics;
- validation errors are visible, associated with fields, and not conveyed by color alone;
- buttons have explicit type/name and disabled/loading states;
- focus-visible indicators remain visible with keyboard navigation;
- interactive cards use semantic buttons/links and have adequate touch targets;
- images have meaningful alt text or are explicitly decorative;
- responsive layouts remain usable at mobile width and 200% zoom;
- non-essential motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
