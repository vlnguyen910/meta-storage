# Accessibility testing

Use `@axe-core/playwright` in Playwright tests. Cover the reservation wizard, customer dashboard, My Storage list/detail, and form-error states at desktop and mobile widths.

Fail on unexpected axe violations. Keep any exclusion local, documented, and tied to a known third-party limitation. Also manually verify keyboard tab order, visible focus, labels, error association, and screen-reader names for buttons/links.
